# Historical experiment harnesses

This directory contains concluded LEFTJeN experiment configurations and NRP
manifests. They remain versioned for provenance and comparison, but are not part
of the supported production surface. Paths and pinned commits may be stale; do
not launch these files without deliberately reconstructing their environment.

The active experiment surface lives in `src/configs/` and `src/nrp/`.

Six Phase-1 A--F manifests and their configs temporarily remain in the active
directories because they had pre-existing uncommitted local edits during this
move. They are compatibility residue, not current recommended experiments.
